# Assignments

[Course home](../README.md) · [Course outline](../COURSE.md) · [Worked exercises](../08_exercises_and_solutions.ipynb)

These assignments turn the examples into something you can explain. They are self-study tasks;
keep your answers in your own notes or a fork. There is no required submission system.

| Assignment | What you produce | Preparation |
|---|---|---|
| [1 · Design a decision](01_design_a_decision.md) | Three questions, criteria, and an action policy | Lessons 00 and 01 |
| [2 · Probabilities and actions](02_probabilities_and_actions.md) | Worked calculations and a cost-based decision | Lessons 02 and 03 |
| [3 · Evaluate a router](03_evaluate_a_router.md) | A short result table, failure explanation, and next evaluation step | Lessons 03 and 07 |

Read the success criteria before you start. When checking an answer, explain why a tempting
alternative would fail. The [practice companion](../PRACTICE.md) and worked notebook exercises
provide supporting explanations.
